About

Care you can feel — and a clinician you can talk to.

Physio's Therapy Touch is the practice of Dr. Hassan: more than five years treating musculoskeletal conditions, sports injuries, back and neck pain, arthritis, and stroke rehabilitation in Lagos.

The work is personal by design. You are not passed between aides. Sessions are one-to-one, explained in plain language, and adjusted to your comfort — including when treatment needs to happen at home.
