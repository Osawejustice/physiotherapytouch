Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

*Do I need a doctor’s referral before seeing a physiotherapist?*
No. You can book an appointment directly with a physiotherapist. However, a referral may be recommended when your condition requires further medical assessment or specialist care.

*Do you offer home physiotherapy services?*
Yes. Home physiotherapy services are available for patients who have difficulty travelling to a clinic or who require treatment in the comfort of their home.

*How many physiotherapy sessions will I need?*
The number of sessions varies depending on your condition, severity, treatment goals, and progress. After your initial assessment, we will recommend an appropriate treatment plan.

*What should I wear for my physiotherapy session?*
Wear comfortable, loose-fitting clothing that allows easy movement and gives access to the area being treated. For lower-limb conditions, shorts or flexible trousers may be helpful.

*Is physiotherapy painful?*
Some treatments may cause mild discomfort, especially when working on stiff or painful areas, but treatment should not cause unnecessary or severe pain. Your treatment will be adjusted according to your comfort and condition.

*Is acupuncture painful?*
Acupuncture generally involves very fine needles, so most people experience little or no pain. You may feel a brief sensation when the needle is inserted, followed by a mild tingling, heaviness, or warmth.

*What conditions can physiotherapy help with?*
Physiotherapy can help manage a wide range of conditions, including back and neck pain, arthritis, sports injuries, joint problems, muscle injuries, stroke-related movement difficulties, post-surgical rehabilitation, and mobility problems.

*Can physiotherapy help with chronic pain?*
Yes. Physiotherapy can help people manage chronic pain by addressing movement limitations, muscle weakness, stiffness, posture, and other contributing factors through an individualized treatment programme.

*What happens during my first physiotherapy session?*
Your first session usually includes an assessment of your symptoms, movement, strength, flexibility, functional limitations, and treatment goals. A personalized treatment plan will then be developed based on the findings.

*How do I book an appointment?*
You can contact us through the booking form, phone, WhatsApp, or other available contact channels to schedule an appointment.
