Care

Treatment for pain, recovery, and everyday movement.

Physiotherapy, manual therapy, acupuncture, and home visits — each session built around your assessment, not a generic protocol.

01 · Physiotherapy

Restore function, then keep it.

Assessment-led treatment for spinal, joint, and muscle problems. Care may include hands-on work, guided exercise, and education so the pain does not simply return next week.

Back pain Neck pain Knee rehab Post-surgery Stroke rehab Sports injury

02 · Manual therapy

Hands on the tissue that will not let go.

Deep tissue massage, relaxation massage, and trigger point therapy to reduce muscle guarding, improve circulation, and prepare the body for movement work.

Deep tissue Relaxation Trigger points

03 · Acupuncture / dry needling

Fine needles, used with purpose.

Acupuncture supports pain management and muscle tension relief. Needles are very fine; most people feel little more than a brief sensation, then warmth or heaviness.

Pain management Muscle tension

04 · Home service

The clinic, without the journey.

Home physiotherapy visits and elderly care rehabilitation across Lagos — for stroke recovery, limited mobility, and anyone who cannot travel comfortably.

Home visits Elderly care
