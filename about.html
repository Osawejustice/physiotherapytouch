About me

Five years treating the pain people live with quietly.

Most people arrive after weeks of “managing it.” They have tried rest, gels, and waiting. Dr. Hassan’s work is to find what the body is protecting — then restore movement with evidence-based physiotherapy, not guesswork.

Sessions are one-to-one. You will know what is being treated, why a technique is used, and what to do between visits. If clinic is difficult, treatment comes to your home.

How care feels

Clinical enough to trust. Human enough to stay with.

Physiotherapy should not feel like a mystery. The first session is an assessment of symptoms, movement, strength, flexibility, functional limits, and your goals. Treatment follows from those findings — not from a standard package.

You may feel mild discomfort when stiff tissue is worked. You should not feel rushed, dismissed, or left without a next step.
