Home service

Physiotherapy that arrives at your door.

For patients who cannot travel comfortably — stroke recovery, post-surgery rehab, elderly care, and anyone who would rather recover in their own living room.

Home service request WhatsApp a request

Lagos-wide

The same clinical standard. A familiar room.

Home visits include assessment, hands-on treatment, and exercises taught to you or your family. Equipment is brought as needed. You do not need a clinic table — a firm bed or clear floor space is enough to begin.

Standard home physiotherapy and elderly care sessions are NGN 60,000. Location and complexity can adjust the fee.

Who it is for

When travelling is the treatment barrier.

Elderly care

Mobility, balance, and strength work at home so independence is protected without a difficult commute.

Stroke recovery

Arm, gait, and functional training in the rooms the patient actually lives in — with family shown how to help.

After surgery

Early rehab when leaving the house is still painful, tiring, or not yet advised.

Busy households

Parents, carers, and professionals who need skilled treatment without losing half a day to traffic.

How to request

Send the area, the condition, and a preferred day.

WhatsApp is the fastest route. Include the Lagos neighbourhood, whether stairs are involved, and who will be present. Dr. Hassan will confirm availability and the session fee before travelling.
